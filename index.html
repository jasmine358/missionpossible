About Pop Out Nation

People of Purpose

Pop Out Nation is a community of People of Purpose. We believe everyone carries gifts, skills, experiences and knowledge that can be used to serve someone else. We help people recognize what they carry, develop it, and put it into action.

Educate•Motivate•Elevate•Empower
Use whatever gift you have received to serve others.1 Peter 4:10
Our vision

To create a space where people discover what they carry, develop it with intention, and activate it in service to others, turning individual purpose into community impact.

Mission Possible

A two-day personal development and purpose-driven experience. Speakers, workshops, conversations and community help you answer one question: what are you carrying, and what are you going to do with it?

Conference pillars

Discover

Discover what you carry. Recognize your gifts, strengths, experiences and possibilities.

Develop

Develop what is already inside of you. Learn, practice, prepare, heal and grow.

Activate

Activate it by putting it into action. Stop waiting for perfect conditions and start moving.

Our Founder

LaTrice Alexander

LaTrice “Trice” Alexander

Founder & Convener • Closing Keynote

LaTrice Alexander is a motivational speaker, entrepreneur, youth mentor and the founder of Pop Out Nation.

Her journey has included speaking, education, mentoring, entrepreneurship, event creation and becoming a professional truck driver. Each chapter shaped the message at the center of her work: your title can change without your purpose disappearing.

Through Pop Out Nation, LaTrice builds spaces that encourage people to recognize their gifts, develop themselves, serve others and take action on the vision inside of them.

The plan can change without the mission changing.
Why it is personal

LaTrice has lived the message. Plans changed. Careers changed. Opportunities changed. Life required pivots and recalculations. The desire to serve, speak, mentor and open doors for others stayed. Mission Possible is built around continuing the mission even when the route changes.

Why a nation

The vision was never meant to stop with one person. Pop Out Nation is a community where everybody is encouraged to use their gift. The goal is not to gather followers. The goal is to activate People of Purpose.
